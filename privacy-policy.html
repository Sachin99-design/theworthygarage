Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Worthy Garage collects when you submit the “Share Your Vehicle Story” form, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the vehicle story form, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your city and state or union territory.
  • Your vehicle model and year.
  • The ownership experience or vehicle story you choose to share.
  • Your WhatsApp/mobile number.
  • Your confirmation that you agree to be contacted about your submission.

Please do not include passwords, financial details, government identity numbers, or other sensitive personal information in your story.

2. Why We Collect This Information

We collect these details for the following limited purposes:

  • To understand your story: Review the vehicle and ownership experience you submit.
  • To find owners by location: Use your city and state to identify potential vehicle stories when our team plans to visit that area.
  • To contact you: Use your WhatsApp/mobile number to discuss your submission or ask for relevant follow-up details.
  • To plan possible features: Assess whether your vehicle or experience may be suitable for a future The Worthy Garage video or other content.
  • To manage submissions: Organise entries and track whether a submission is new, contacted, or filmed.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Your information is reviewed by The Worthy Garage team for the purposes described above.
  • Your city and state may be used to filter and organise submissions for location-based planning.
  • We may contact you using the WhatsApp/mobile number you provide, provided you have agreed to be contacted.
  • Submitting a story does not guarantee that your vehicle will be selected, contacted, visited, or featured.

4. Publicity and Video Features

Submitting this form does not give us permission to publish your name, phone number, private contact details, or story automatically. If we are interested in featuring you or your vehicle, we will discuss the proposed feature with you separately. Any agreement needed for filming or publication will be obtained separately before we proceed.

5. Storage and Access

Submitted information may be stored in a Google Sheet or another digital system used by The Worthy Garage to manage vehicle story submissions. Access is intended to be limited to authorised people working on this activity. We do not display the submission database or other viewers’ contact details on the public form.

Digital services can experience outages or security incidents. We take reasonable steps to restrict access, but no online storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing Your Information

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not make your WhatsApp/mobile number or private submission details publicly available.
  • Information may be processed by service providers, such as Google, where needed to host the form or store submissions.
  • We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a lawful request.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep submissions only for as long as reasonably needed to review and manage vehicle stories, plan possible features, maintain submission records, or meet legal requirements. You can contact us to request deletion of your submission, subject to any legal or operational reason that requires us to retain it.

8. Your Choice and Requests

  • Submitting the form is voluntary.
  • You may choose not to submit the form if you do not agree with this policy.
  • You may ask us to correct or delete the information you submitted.
  • You may ask us not to contact you further about your submission.

To make a request, email support@theworthygarage.com and mention “Vehicle Story Privacy Request” in the subject. Please provide enough information for us to identify your submission; do not send sensitive identity documents unless we specifically explain why they are necessary.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the use of your submission, contact:

  • The Worthy Garage
  • Email: support@theworthygarage.com
  • WhatsApp/Phone: +91 76794 82290
Please note: This policy describes the intended handling of information submitted through the vehicle story form. Make sure your actual Google Sheets sharing permissions and Apps Script deployment access are restricted to your authorised account before publishing the form.
